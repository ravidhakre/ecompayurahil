Everything you might want to know about our books, paper, shipping, payments and returns. Still stuck? We are one message away.

What makes Arts Book books different?
Every book uses 100 GSM bleed-resistant paper, single-sided printing and original line art drawn in our studio. That combination means you can use colour pencils, gel pens and most markers without the next illustration spoiling your work. The binding also lies flat, which makes colouring near the spine much easier.
Are the books suitable for adults or children?
Both. Our Mandala Magic, Stress Relief, Floral Dreams, Travel Diaries and Fantasy Worlds collections are designed with adults in mind. The Little Artists collection uses bolder, simpler outlines for children aged roughly 4–8. Every product page lists a recommended age group and page count.
Can I see the inside pages before buying?
Yes. Every product page shows the front cover plus all 25 real inside pages. Click any page to open a larger preview. What you see is the actual artwork printed in the book, not a mock-up.
What is the paper weight and book size?
Most titles are A4 size (210 × 297 mm) and use 100 GSM acid-free paper. The Little Artists range may have a different page count, which is listed on the product page. Pages are printed single-sided so you always colour on a clean sheet.
Will markers bleed through the paper?
The paper handles colour pencils and gel pens beautifully, and most light marker work without ghosting. Alcohol-based markers can still bleed through if you lay down a lot of ink. We recommend placing a spare sheet behind the page if you use those, or sticking to pencils and gel pens.

Orders placed before 4:00 PM on a working day are usually dispatched within 24 hours. Orders placed on Sundays or public holidays are dispatched on the next working day. You will receive a tracking update by email and SMS once the parcel leaves our studio.
How long does delivery take?
Metro cities typically receive delivery in 2–4 working days. Other serviceable pin codes usually take 4–7 working days. Remote or North-East locations can take slightly longer. These are estimates from our courier partners, not guarantees.

Online payments are processed by PayU and include UPI, credit cards, debit cards, net banking and popular wallets. We also accept Cash on Delivery. All online payments use an encrypted, PCI-DSS compliant gateway; Arts Book never stores your card details.
