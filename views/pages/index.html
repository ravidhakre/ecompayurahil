The Art Guru difference

Made for real colourists.

We obsess over the things you notice only when you sit down to colour: paper weight, line quality, page layout and whether your marker bleeds through.

Thick 100 GSM paper

Bleed-resistant pages that handle colour pencils, gel pens and light marker work without ghosting through.

Original line art

Drawn by real artists, with confident clean lines and just the right amount of detail for a satisfying session.

Single-sided pages

Each design sits on its own page, so your finished artwork never gets spoiled by the next illustration.

Made in India

Designed, printed and packed by a small team in Bengaluru. Your order supports independent creative work.
