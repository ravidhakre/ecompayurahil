2. Cancellation Policy (Duration: 24 hours)

Customers may request cancellation of an order within 24 hours of placing the order. To help us process the request quickly, please contact us as soon as possible with your order number.

  • How to cancel: Email {{EMAIL}} (subject: “Cancel order [order number]”) or call/WhatsApp {{PHONE}} during {{SUPPORT_HOURS}}.
  • If cancelled within 24 hours and before dispatch: the order will be cancelled and a full refund (including shipping/COD charges, if applicable) will be initiated as per the refund mode & timeline below.
  • If the order has already been dispatched/handed to the courier: cancellation may not be possible. In such cases, you may refuse delivery (where feasible) or raise an eligible return/refund request after delivery under the Return & Refund Policy below.
  • Note: For COD orders, repeated refusal without a valid reason may affect future COD availability.

3. Return & Refund Policy (Duration: {{RETURNS_DAYS}} days)

You may raise a return/refund request within {{RETURNS_DAYS}} days of delivery in eligible cases, such as:

  • Product received is damaged, defective, misprinted, or has missing pages;
  • Wrong product/title/quantity received; or
  • Product is materially different from the description shown on the website.

Not eligible (unless defective/damaged on arrival): items that have been used/coloured/written on, damaged after delivery due to mishandling, or items marked non-returnable on the product page (this does not affect rights for defective/wrong items).

How to submit a return/refund request

  1. Contact us within {{RETURNS_DAYS}} days of delivery at {{EMAIL}} or {{PHONE}}.
  2. Share your order number, item details, reason for return/refund, and clear photos/videos (especially for damaged/wrong items).
  3. Our team will review the request and confirm eligibility and next steps (pickup/replacement/refund) as applicable.

4. Refund mode & timeline

  • Refund mode: Once the refund request is submitted and approved, the refund amount will be returned via the same payment mode/method used by the customer for the original transaction (e.g. UPI/card/net banking/wallet used at checkout), wherever technically supported by the payment provider/bank.
  • Refund timeline: The refund will be processed within 7 days of the refund request submission/approval as per policy. The actual credit time may vary slightly depending on the bank, payment gateway (PayU), or card issuer timelines.
  • Cash on Delivery (COD) orders: Since payment is collected in cash, approved refunds for COD orders will be processed to the customer’s bank/UPI details shared with us (after verification). We will process such refunds within 7 days of request submission/approval.
  • Cancelled orders (prepaid): Eligible prepaid cancellations/refunds will be returned to the original payment method.

If the refund is due to our error (damaged/defective/wrong item) or eligible cancellation before dispatch, we refund the full eligible amount as per policy. In cases of failed/duplicate transactions, the amount will be verified and refunded/reversed as applicable.
