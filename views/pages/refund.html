Short version: Cancel free before dispatch. Report damaged, defective or wrong books within {{RETURNS_DAYS}} days of delivery with photos and we will replace or refund them. Coloured-in books cannot be returned unless defective.

1. Cancelling an order

You can cancel your order free of charge at any time before it is dispatched. To cancel:

  1. Email {{EMAIL}} with the subject “Cancel order [your order number]”, or call {{PHONE}} during {{SUPPORT_HOURS}}.
  2. If the order has not yet been packed, we will cancel it and refund the full amount paid.
  3. If it has already been dispatched, you can no longer cancel it. You may still request a return after delivery if the book qualifies under section 3.

COD orders can be cancelled before dispatch in the same way. Refusing a COD parcel at delivery is not treated as a cancellation and may lead to COD being disabled for future orders.

2. Return window

You may request a return within {{RETURNS_DAYS}} days of delivery if:

  • the book arrives damaged, torn, misprinted or with missing pages;
  • you received the wrong title, quantity or edition; or
  • the book is materially different from its description on the website.

Requests made after {{RETURNS_DAYS}} days may be declined, except where the law requires otherwise.

3. What cannot be returned

  • Books that have been coloured in, written on, cut, torn or otherwise used, unless the defect was present at delivery.
  • Books damaged after delivery due to mishandling, water, heat or improper storage.
  • Books bought during a final sale or clearance where the product page clearly said “non-returnable”. This does not affect your rights if the item is defective.
  • Free gifts, swatch cards and promotional items, unless they arrived damaged.

This is a copyright-sensitive product, so we cannot resell used books. We appreciate your understanding.

4. How to request a return or replacement

  1. Email {{EMAIL}} within {{RETURNS_DAYS}} days of delivery.
  2. Include your order number, the title affected, a short description of the issue, and clear photos of the book and outer packaging.
  3. Our team will review the request and reply within 2 working days with the next steps.
  4. If approved, we will arrange a pickup where serviceable, or ask you to courier the book back to our studio. Do not send anything back before receiving our confirmation.
  5. Once the book is received and inspected, we will process a replacement or refund as agreed.

5. Refund timelines

Bank processing times are outside our control. If a refund has not appeared after 10 working days, contact us with your order number and we will provide the refund reference.

6. Shipping and COD fees on refunds

If the return is due to our error (damaged, defective or wrong item), we refund the full amount paid, including shipping and COD fees, and cover the return shipping cost. If you cancel before dispatch, we refund the full amount. For returns requested for other approved reasons, original shipping and COD handling fees are non-refundable.

7. Failed or duplicate payments

If your bank account was debited but the order was not confirmed, the payment may have failed at the gateway. Such amounts are usually reversed automatically within 5–7 working days. If not, email us with your order number, payment reference and a screenshot of the debit. If you were charged twice for the same order, we will refund the duplicate amount in full once verified.

8. Chargebacks

Please contact us before raising a chargeback with your bank. Most issues can be resolved faster by our team. If a chargeback is raised, we may share order and delivery records with PayU and the bank to verify the transaction.

9. Damaged in transit

If the outer parcel looks damaged, please photograph it before opening and, if possible, refuse delivery. Report the damage to us within 48 hours. We will arrange a replacement or refund under section 5. Please keep the original packaging until the claim is resolved.
